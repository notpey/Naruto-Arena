How a match works

Naruto-Arena is a turn-based strategy game. You bring a team of three characters into battle and spend chakra each turn to activate their skills.

Every skill has a cooldown, a set of classes (Physical, Instant, Melee, and so on), and an chakra nature it requires. Reading those values — shown on every character's detail page — is the core of building a winning team.

Browse the Characters & Skills tab to study each fighter's kit before you queue up. Choose Start Playing when you're ready to put a team together.
