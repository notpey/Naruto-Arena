Clan Ladder

The Clan Ladder is Naruto-Arena's international clan ranking. Clans join automatically as their members play clan ladder games — every member's result contributes to the clan's standing, so the strongest, most active alliances rise to the top.

/ Seasonal Competition

Road to Hokage

Road to Hokage is Naruto-Arena's seasonal competitive mode. Each season resets the standings and gives every shinobi a fresh climb toward the top — rewarding consistency across a full season rather than a single hot streak, with refined matchmaking and seasonal progression on top of standard ladder play.

What is a season?
A fixed competitive period. When it ends, final standings are recorded and the ladder resets so everyone starts the next season even.
How is it different from the Ninja Ladder?
The Ninja Ladder is a continuous, all-time individual ranking. Road to Hokage is season-scoped, with its own progression and end-of-season recognition.
What do I earn?
Seasonal progression and a place in history — the top 10 of every season are immortalized in the Hall of Fame.

Current season standings appear on the Ninja Ladder above.
